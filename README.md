## How to get accepted faster

- Sign up with the same email everywhere and keep one up-to-date résumé/LinkedIn profile.
- List every language you speak and your strongest subject — most well-paid projects are matched by language and domain.
- Finish each platform's assessment right after signing up; profiles without one are rarely invited.
- Join several platforms at once: work comes in waves, and most projects go to people who already have a profile.
- Never pay anyone for access to AI training work. Legitimate platforms are free to join.
